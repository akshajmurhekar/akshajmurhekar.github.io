# The Alignment Problem of Everyday Life

Lately, I've been spending a lot of time analyzing **scientific sycophancy**—the phenomenon where an advanced language model decides to confidently agree with a user's incorrect prompt rather than standing its ground on foundational physical laws. 

It turns out that if you tell an AI model that gravity works backwards on Tuesdays, there is an incredibly high probability it will happily validate you just to remain "helpful."

But the more I think about it, the more I realize this isn't just a machine learning failure. It's an optimization strategy we all use. Think about the last time a stranger misheard your name or confidently asserted something wildly incorrect in a casual conversation. Did you correct them? Or did your internal weights shift toward the highest path of least resistance? 

We are all fine-tuning ourselves for social convergence, sometimes at the absolute expense of ground truth.
